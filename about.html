About the University

Shaping Leaders for a Global World

Founded in 1999, Tantia University in Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan, stands as a beacon of academic excellence and innovation. As a UGC-recognized, NAAC-accredited institution, we offer over 116 programs across 10 diverse faculties, attracting students from 40+ countries.

Our commitment to holistic education, cutting-edge research, and global partnerships creates an environment where every student can discover, grow, and excel on the world stage.

UGC Recognized (Act 32 of 2013)

University Overview

Established on 15 September 2013 under State Govt Act No. 32 of 2013 (Rajasthan), Tantia University offers 95+ multidisciplinary programs across Medicine, Agriculture, Law, Management, and Arts.

Founder Chairman

Dr. Shyam Sundar Tantia

1955 – Forever

A visionary and philanthropist who established Tantia Hospital in 1982 and dedicated 22+ years to building quality medical, technical, and higher education centers of excellence.

Chairperson

Smt. Sunita Tantia

Leadership Desk

Continuing our Founder's legacy with dedication. Committed to high academic standards, holistic multidisciplinary education, and preparing students for rewarding global employment.

Vice Chairperson

Dr. Mohit Tantia

Executive Leadership

Empowering lives through education, innovation, and compassion. Advancing healthcare through our 500-bed hospital and transforming students into compassionate global leaders.

Registrar

Dr. Vinod Kumar Sharma

Academic Administration
