International Students

Dedicated Support for
Global Students

We make your transition to India smooth, safe, and exciting with comprehensive international student services.

✈️
Airport & Travel Assistance
We arrange airport pickup, temporary accommodation, and orientation to help you settle in from day one.
🛂
Visa & Immigration Help
Dedicated visa counselors assist with student visa applications, FRO registration, and visa extensions.
🗣️
Language Support
English-medium instruction, Hindi language classes for international students, and multilingual student mentors.
💰
Scholarship Counseling
Expert guidance on international scholarships, Study in India scheme, and university merit awards.
🏥
Healthcare & Insurance
University hospital for student health, medical insurance guidance, and 24/7 emergency medical support.
🌐
Cultural Integration
International student cell organizing cultural exchange, local tours, and buddy programs with Indian students.
