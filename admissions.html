How to Apply

Simple 4-Step Admission Process

International and domestic admissions made simple. Follow these steps to join Tantia University.

📋
Check Eligibility
Review program requirements. International students need AIU-recognized qualifications and valid passport.
📝
Fill Application
Complete the online application form with personal, academic, and program preference details.
📄
Submit Documents
Upload transcripts, English proficiency scores (IELTS/TOEFL), passport copy, and SOP.
🎓
Get Admission
Receive provisional letter, pay fees, process student visa, and prepare for your global journey.
Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

🌍 International Students

From SAARC, African, Middle East & other countries

📚
AIU-recognized 10+2 or equivalent qualification
🌐
IELTS 6.0+ or TOEFL 80+ (English proficiency)
🛂
Valid passport with student visa eligibility
📋
Minimum 50% in relevant subjects (varies by program)
💳
Apply via Study in India portal or direct admission

🇮🇳 Domestic Students

Indian students from all states

🎓
10+2 (PCB for Medical / PCM for Engineering) from recognized board
📊
Minimum 50% aggregate marks in qualifying exam
🏆
NEET score (for MBBS, BAMS, BHMS programs)
📑
JEE Main / REAP for Engineering programs
🆔
Valid Aadhaar, domicile certificate & mark sheets
