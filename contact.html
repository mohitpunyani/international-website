FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can international students apply to Tantia University? +
International students can apply through the university's official website or the Study in India portal. The process involves eligibility review, submission of documents (transcripts, English proficiency scores, passport), fee payment, and visa processing.
What English proficiency tests are accepted? +
We accept IELTS (minimum 6.0 overall), TOEFL (minimum 80 iBT), Cambridge English qualifications, and PTE Academic. Students from English-medium schools may be exempt with appropriate documentation.
Are there scholarships available for international students? +
Yes! We offer the International Scholar Award (50% tuition waiver), Merit Scholarship (up to 100%), and Sports Excellence Grant (25%). Students can also apply for the Study in India Government Scholarship.
What is the fee for MBBS for international students? +
MBBS tuition fees for international students are approximately $14,500 USD per year. Hostel and mess facilities are an additional $1,500 per year. Contact admissions for a complete fee breakdown.
Does Tantia University help with student visa? +
Absolutely. Our international cell provides full support including provisional admission letters for visa applications, FRRO registration guidance, visa extension support, and immigration counseling throughout your stay.
Is Tantia University recognized internationally? +
Yes. Tantia University is UGC-recognized, NAAC accredited, and an AIU member — meaning our degrees are recognized by universities and professional bodies worldwide. MBBS is MCI/NMC approved, and Law is BCI recognized.
